About Priyesh Thakare

DRISNOVA was founded by Priyesh Thakare, an engineering professional with more than 12 years of experience in industrial machine vision and engineering solutions.

His professional journey includes four years of full-time work experience in the United States, along with extensive exposure to international engineering environments across North America, Europe and Asia.

Throughout his 12+ year engineering career, he has contributed to more than 1,000 machine vision applications and developed more than 1,000 proof-of-concept (POC) and technical solutions across complex shop-floor manufacturing environments.

His expertise includes industrial machine vision, vision programming, AI-based inspection, robotics integration, application feasibility, system commissioning and technical problem-solving.

With a strong passion for engineering and entrepreneurship, he established DRISNOVA to bring practical industrial experience, advanced technologies and customer-focused engineering solutions together under one platform.

I believe the future of manufacturing belongs to companies that can combine engineering intelligence with practical innovation.
