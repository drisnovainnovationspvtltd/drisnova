About DRISNOVA

Engineering Vision. Empowering Automation. We build intelligent, reliable, and measurable vision systems for global manufacturing industries.

Pioneering Machine Vision & Intelligent Automation

DRISNOVA was established with a clear vision: to make advanced industrial vision and intelligent automation more accessible, practical and reliable for manufacturing industries.

We specialize in industrial machine vision, AI-based inspection, robotic vision integration and engineering consultation.

Our approach combines technical knowledge, real-world industrial experience and a problem-solving mindset to develop solutions that address actual manufacturing challenges. Whether it is a new vision application, an existing inspection system that needs improvement, or a complex robotic vision requirement, we work towards delivering solutions that create measurable value.

Brand Etymology

The Meaning Behind DRISNOVA

DRISHTI (दृष्टि • Vision) + NOVA (New Innovation)

DRISHTI (दृष्टि) is the timeless Sanskrit word for Vision, Perception, and Sight. Combined with NOVA, signifying a radiant new star and technological innovation, DRISNOVA embodies our belief in combining vision, discovery, technology and innovation to create smarter industrial possibilities.

Beyond Conventional Automation

DRISNOVA stands for moving beyond conventional automation and building a future where intelligent machines can see, understand and act. It represents our commitment to continuous innovation, engineering excellence and technological advancement.
