Industrial Domain Expertise

Intelligent Vision Across Industries

Our engineering capabilities can support a wide range of manufacturing and industrial applications. Our focus is simple: understand the application, identify the right technology and engineer a reliable solution.

Automotive & Auto Components

Powertrain subassemblies, EV battery module verification, brake disc inspection, weld seam checks, connector pin alignment, and DPM traceability.

Electrical & Electronics

PCB solder joint inspection, SMD component presence/polarity, semiconductor packaging, harness assembly verification, and microscopic defect classification.

Engineering & Precision Manufacturing

Micron-level dimensional measurement, concentricity, runout, thread pitch inspection, tool wear monitoring, and GD&T tolerance compliance.

Metal Fabrication

Stamping burr and tear checks, surface scratch and dent inspection on sheet metal, robotic weld seam tracking, and laser cut profile verification.

Packaging & Consumer Goods

Label skew and misprint checks, cap position and seal integrity verification, barcode readability, carton fill level, and high-speed packaging sortation.

Pharma & Medical Device

Blister pack integrity, broken tablet detection, vial crimp inspection, needle straightness verification, syringe graduations, and 21 CFR Part 11 OCR serialization.

Food & Beverage Processing

Bottle fill level, closure seal integrity, foreign body detection, expiration date batch printing validation, and color-based classification on conveyors.

Industrial Equipment

Heavy machinery sub-assemblies, hydraulic component verification, bolt tightening confirmation, chassis orientation, and robotic cell vision coordination.
