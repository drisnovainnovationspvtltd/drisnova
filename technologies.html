Working With Advanced Industrial Technologies

We work with established industrial vision, sensing and robotics platforms to identify the right technology for every application. Technology selection is application-driven.

Platform Tier 01

Machine Vision Platforms

COGNEX In-Sight
In-Sight, VisionPro & Industrial Solutions

Deep experience programming Cognex In-Sight smart cameras, spreadsheet-based logic, patMax pattern matching, edge detection, and VisionPro deep learning integration.

KEYENCE IV Series
IV Series, XG-X & CV-X Vision Systems

Expert configuration of Keyence vision sensors, high-speed multi-camera controllers, ultra-high resolution line scan systems, and auto-focus optical units.

OMRON FH Series
Industrial Vision & Sensor Solutions

Implementation of Omron FH and FZ series vision controllers with seamless Sysmac PLC EtherCAT communication and high-speed defect screening.

HIKVISION Robotics
Machine Vision Cameras & Smart Algorithms

Deployment of area-scan cameras, GigE/USB3 interfaces, smart code readers, and high-performance algorithmic inspection for cost-sensitive automation.

FANUC iRVision
Robot & Integrated Vision Solutions

Complete integration of FANUC iRVision 2D and 3D camera systems for automated part picking, tray loading, and dynamic robot guidance.

Platform Tier 02

Robotics & 3D Vision

FANUC Robotics

Kinematics integration, tool center point (TCP) calibration, coordinate matrix translation between camera frame and robot base frame, and cycle-time optimization.

MECH-MIND 3D

Industrial structured-light 3D cameras, Mech-Eye hardware, point cloud processing, collision-free trajectory planning, and complex unstructured bin picking.

Robot-Guided Vision

Fixed camera, arm-mounted camera, and hybrid vision architectures for real-time part orientation correction, automated assembly, and palletizing.

Platform Tier 03

Other Technologies & Industrial Hardware

Industrial Cameras

High-speed monochrome, color, rolling & global shutter area-scan and line-scan sensors.

Smart Cameras

All-in-one embedded processing cameras with onboard I/O and industrial networking.

Fixed Industrial Scanners

High-throughput 1D/2D barcode, QR and Direct Part Mark (DPM) laser and image readers.

Handheld Barcode Scanners

Rugged industrial wireless and tethered scanners for factory floor verification.

PROFINET, Ethernet/IP, Modbus TCP, EtherCAT, CC-Link, Serial RS-232/485, and digital discrete I/O.

Technology Selection is Application-Driven

We recommend solutions based on inspection requirements, accuracy, cycle time, environment and budget. We never push a one-size-fits-all hardware vendor; we analyze your part's optical characteristics and select the exact sensor and optics combination that delivers flawless production stability.
