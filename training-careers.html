Talent & Capability Development

Building the Next Generation of Vision Engineers

The future of manufacturing depends on engineers who understand intelligent machines. DRISNOVA aims to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical industrial machine vision applications.

Industrial Curriculum

Practical Training Programs

Hands-on modules structured around real factory hardware, optical setups, programming tools, and actual production inspection scenarios.

1. Machine Vision Fundamentals

Core concepts of digital image formation, resolution, pixel depth, shutter types (global vs rolling), and industrial vision system architectures.

2. Industrial Camera Setup

Mounting considerations, sensor selection, GigE Vision & USB3 Vision protocols, triggering methods, and hardware exposure configuration.

3. Lens & Lighting Selection

Optical calculations (FOV, WD, focal length), telecentric optics, darkfield/brightfield illumination, polarization, and dome lighting for glare reduction.

4. Vision Programming

Algorithm formulation, edge detection, blob analysis, pattern matching, OCR/OCV, calibration tools, and industrial PLC/robot communication setup.

5. Industrial Inspection Projects

Hands-on capstone projects resolving real defect cases: automotive assembly checks, pharma blister packaging, and electronics solder joint inspection.

6. Advanced Vision Applications

Deep-learning inspection classification, 3D point-cloud processing, robotic guidance kinematics, and GR&R statistical measurement validation.

Who Can Join?

🚀 Fresh Graduates looking for specialized automation careers
⚙️ Working Automation & PLC Engineers wanting to add Vision to their toolkit
🔍 Manufacturing & Quality Engineers responsible for zero-defect production
